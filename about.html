About Desi Dastarkhwan

Food tastes even better when it feels familiar.

Our little story

Good food, without the fuss.

Desi Dastarkhwan is built around a simple idea: a satisfying meal can make an ordinary day feel special. Our menu brings together much-loved Pakistani dishes, from fragrant rice meals to rich, comforting curries.

We believe in familiar flavours, generous hospitality and keeping things simple. Whether you are in the mood for biryani or a plate of daal chawal, we hope you find something that feels just right.
